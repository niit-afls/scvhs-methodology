# Contributing to SCVHS

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the SCVHS methodology.

## Who Can Contribute

SCVHS is a proprietary methodology developed by NIIT Limited. Contributions to this repository are accepted from:
- NIIT Limited employees and contractors
- NIIT instructional design partners with a signed contribution agreement

External contributions are not accepted at this time.

## What Contributions Are Accepted

- **New worked examples** for courses not yet covered (C07–C09 examples are currently sparse)
- **Corrections to existing examples** where a Decision Log entry is inaccurate or a spec is ambiguous
- **New Comprehension Primitive examples** for technologies not yet listed in `02-comprehension-primitive.md`
- **Sprint Design Checklist additions** based on delivery experience
- **Clarifications to the research paper** — additional citations, corrections to existing citations

## What Is Not Open for External Contribution

- The five-phase pipeline specification (Section 4 of the research paper)
- The three-mode structure and their definitions
- The Comprehension Primitive concept and specification
- The Decision Log concept, structure, and specification
- The Scaffolding Fade model

These constitute the core of the proprietary methodology. Changes are made only by NIIT's instructional design team.

## How to Propose a Contribution

1. Contact the NIIT instructional design team with a description of the proposed contribution.
2. Receive a contributor agreement to sign.
3. Submit the contribution as a pull request or document review.
4. All contributions are reviewed by the methodology lead before acceptance.

## Style Standards

All contributions must comply with the SCVHS style standards:
- No em-dashes — use hyphens
- Semicolons as list delimiters in CTKS documents
- "Construct" not "block" as the unit term
- "An AI tool" not a specific tool name
- Phase headers use SCVHS terms: SPECIFY, CONSTRUCT, VALIDATE, HARDEN, SHIP
- Decision Log entries are specific — name the construct, name the spec reference, name the defect
